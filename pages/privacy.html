Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 January 2025 · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Summary

CytherAI does not track you. This site uses no analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts, and no fingerprinting. We receive only what you choose to send us by email, including the draft the contact page prepares in your own email app.

1. Data We Collect

The contact page: the three answers you write there (what system you are working on, what you would want AI to do, and what must never happen) stay in your browser; the page neither sends nor stores them. “Open email draft” hands them to your own email app as a draft (the mailto: protocol), and they reach us only if you send that email, with the address and name your mail account adds. “Copy the message” puts the same text on your device’s clipboard and nowhere else. Loading the page is logged by the host like any other page (see below); your answers are not part of that request.

Direct email: If you email us directly, we retain your correspondence for the purpose of responding and managing the engagement relationship.

Engagement data: Information shared during capability conversations, NDA briefings, or supervised review sessions is handled under the applicable confidentiality agreement.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No cookies
  • No analytics or tracking pixels
  • No third-party scripts or beacons
  • No browser fingerprinting
  • No IP address logging at the application level

One thing may be written to your own device, and it is neither transmitted nor readable by us: the service worker keeps a copy of this site’s own files so that the site works offline; it holds no personal data and is removed by clearing site data. (Until 20 October 2026 the homepage could remember a light or dark choice in your browser; it no longer does, and nothing reads a choice saved before then. Clearing this site’s data removes it.)

This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes; that applies to every page of this site, the contact page included. CytherAI does not use these logs for tracking or profiling.

3. How We Use Your Data

Personal data you send us by email, from the contact page’s draft or directly, is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Evaluate fit for a potential engagement
  • Manage an ongoing engagement relationship

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Data Retention

Email correspondence, including messages sent from the contact page’s draft, is retained for the duration of any business relationship plus two years, unless a longer period is required by law or a specific engagement agreement.

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing contact@cytherai.com.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal data in our possession. For sensitive communications, PGP-encrypted email is available upon request. See our security policy for vulnerability reporting.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data held by CytherAI. To exercise these rights, contact contact@cytherai.com.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date above.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@cytherai.com
