CytherCAD — Controlled Generative CAD

Compact autoregressive model producing executable parametric construction programs. Constrained decoding restricts invalid geometry at generation time. Kernel-backed verification checks the geometry after generation.

SijilOS — Offline-First Ledger

Transactional ledger for the Saudi market, running on the device and fully offline-capable, with no cloud service at its core. It produces the QR code that ZATCA’s Phase 1 e-invoicing rules require. No certification, deployment or pilot is claimed.
