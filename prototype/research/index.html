01Research agenda

A capable model is half a system.

Models are probabilistic; consequences are not. Our research is the other half: what a model may generate, what checks its work from outside, what can refuse it, and what keeps the record. These are shared questions. Each system answers the ones its domain needs, and Table 1 shows which, with sources.

PRINCIPLE 01

Bound before you ask.

Define what a system may do before asking what a model can do.

PRINCIPLE 02

Check from outside.

Whatever decides must not be the thing that proposed. A model never approves itself.

PRINCIPLE 03

Make stopping a result.

Refuse, block and escalate are outcomes with reasons, not failures to hide.

02Findings

Four questions, four kinds of consequence.

Each entry states the question, the method, what was observed, where it stops, and the artifact that supports it. Maturity is given as it stands.

R-01CytherCAD GeometryProduction-validated

Can a generator be kept from emitting invalid geometry while it generates, rather than filtered afterwards?

Method
A compact autoregressive model (16.1M parameters) produces executable parametric construction programs. Constrained decoding enforces geometric validity at generation time; kernel-backed verification confirms dimensional accuracy afterwards.
Observation
0.00% invalid outputs on Text2CAD-192, against 10.00% for DeepCAD and 0.93% for Text2CAD, as published (Fig. 2).
Limitation
Valid is not the same as intended: dimensional accuracy is 99% within ±3 mm and multi-operation accuracy 91%, both on n = 35. General text-to-CAD semantic parity is not claimed. Text2CAD-192 is published by others; we do not operate it.
Artifact
Validation summary v1.1, April 2026 · Exhibit A, CAD-2024-001
R-02ADII DataResearch

When a number in a real system looks wrong, who may change it?

Method
An investigator agent reads through a read-only tool layer and proposes an answer: fix it, leave it, or escalate it. The AI that proposes a fix cannot approve it. Two programs outside the model decide whether the change is allowed, and whether it holds.
Observation
Demonstrated end to end on a recorded fix, replayed.
Limitation
A research project. No evaluation results are published, and no deployment is claimed.
Artifact
ADII running, a recorded fix replayed, 2026

R-03AWC-OS MoneyEvaluation · AMAD 2026

Can an AI’s financial answer be stopped when it is not authorized, by something other than the model?

Method
A fine-tuned model writes a financial program. A canonical deterministic interpreter executes it, refuses it under a constitutional predicate (offering a compliant alternative), or blocks it when an assertion fails, with a replayable receipt either way.
Observation
A measured failure, retained: one savings prompt generated an over-promising takaful program. Its assertion failed and the engine returned BLOCKED. No rule was relaxed and no output rewritten to force a pass.
Limitation
Evaluation stage. The live demonstrator runs on an evaluation machine at 4–8 seconds per question. Success is measured as unauthorized answers failing safely, not as an answer every time.
Artifact
Evaluation gateway: film, deck and source, AMAD 2026
Exhibit · Fig. 1c

Step through an illustrated takaful failure.

An over-promising program like the recorded one, run line by line on a small illustrative engine, not the canonical interpreter. Watch its own assertion stop it.

R-04SijilOS RecordsLive beta · 2 environments

Can a financial ledger keep how every record came to be, and do it with no network?

Method
An event-sourced transactional ledger for the Saudi market. Intent is stored over time as an immutable log, and current state is derived from it. A correction is a new event; history is never edited.
Observation
ZATCA Phase 1: certified compliant, April 2025. Live beta in two production environments. Offline-first, with zero core cloud dependency.
Limitation
The certification is ZATCA Phase 1; the system is in live beta.
Artifact
Operational record · Exhibit B, SIJ-2025-001
Exhibit · Fig. 1d

Try to edit history.

Submit intents to an append-only ledger, try to rewrite a past event, then replay the whole ledger from genesis.
