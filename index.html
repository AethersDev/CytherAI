01 · ADII

A number looks wrong.
Changing it is a separate decision.

ADII investigates the number and answers: fix it, leave it, or escalate it.

The AI that proposes a fix can’t approve it. Two programs outside the model decide whether the change is allowed and whether it holds.

A research project: no evaluation results are published and no deployment is claimed.

02 · AWC-OS

Not every answer
is allowed.

The AI proposes a financial program. A separate engine checks it against the rules: it can run, be refused with an allowed alternative, or be blocked.

In the demonstration, a proposal was blocked when it failed its own condition. The rules stayed in place.

A demonstration: the MVP runs on an evaluation machine, not on deployed hosting.

03 · CytherCAD

Language in.
Valid geometry out.

Describe a part. CytherCAD turns that description into instructions that build it, restricts invalid geometry during generation, and checks the dimensions afterwards.

0.00% invalid rate on Text2CAD-192, against 10.00% for DeepCAD, an external benchmark CytherAI does not operate.

Runs on-prem, private and air-gapped.
