SHEET 2 · THE SYSTEM

Validity is a property of the boundary, not of the model.

CytherCAD generates CAD from text. The model only proposes. A boundary admits or refuses every token against the grammar and then the geometry; an independent kernel re-verifies the finished program. What cannot pass never becomes an output — it is refused at the boundary, not filtered afterwards.

A second system is indexed and sealed: a zero-dependency neural theorem prover, reference NTP-2025-001. Its contents open under NDA.
