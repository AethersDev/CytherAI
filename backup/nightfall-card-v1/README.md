# nightfall-card-v1 — the link previews with the film's last frame, retired

The English link-preview card of the Nightfall release (2026-11-23): the landscape rooftop poster, the darkening the front door sets
behind its statement, the logo and *Let AI change things. Keep control of what changes.*, rendered by `tools/render-og-film.py` from
`tools/og-card.html`, which is here at the path it had. It was replaced the same day by the identity kit's Open Graph image, copied byte
for byte (IMG-031). The Arabic card, of the same composition with the statement in Arabic (`tools/og-card-ar.html`, `tools/render-og-film.py ar`),
was served until 2026-11-24, when the kit's card with its line in Arabic replaced it (IMG-032); it is here too, with its composition.
Their receipts, IMG-028 and IMG-029, stand in `docs/asset-promotion-log.md` with their output paths moved here, and
`tools/test-assets.py` still verifies these bytes against them.
Nothing here is served.
