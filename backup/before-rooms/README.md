# before-rooms — the ADII page and the contact pages as served until 2026-10-21

`adii/index.html`, `contact.html` and `ar/contact.html`, byte for byte as the live build `73B4CD7CEF71A0F3` served them
(commit `fdb58c3`). ADII's page kept the light design of its first publication (`backup/project-pages/adii/`) with a
build stamp, a status line and a route home; the contact pages were the cold-paper subpages' (`css/cytherai.css`): a
"communication protocol", name, email, organisation, message and a consent box, posted to `mailto:`.

Superseded on 2026-10-21, at the owner's direction, by rooms of the front door (`css/room.css`): the evidence's arrival
(what the recorded run is, what decision it shows and what it does not claim, beside the run), an Arabic introduction
(`ar/adii.html`), and a contact page that continues *Tell us what must never happen.* with three short answers for the
visitor's own mail app. The recorded run, its text and the team with their CVs are carried over verbatim. Their links
were relative to their served paths, so open them at `fdb58c3` to run them. Not served, precached or named by any served
page.
