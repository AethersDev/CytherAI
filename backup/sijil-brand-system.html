01 — PRIMARY VARIATIONS

Core Seal Expressions

Six approaches to the seal form, each maintaining the register's authority while adjusting visual weight and context.

Full Seal

Primary mark. Octagonal frame with running doctrine text and full Arabic wordmark. Default for all formal applications.

Minimal Circle

Stripped to the essential: circle and wordmark. Favicons, small-scale digital, embossing.

Geometric Frame

Precise octagonal container with axis construction lines. Technical, architectural feel. Print headers.

Document Stamp

Rectangular official stamp. Letterheads, formal documents, report headers. Closest to physical register format.

Diamond Seal

Rotated square — the physical form of a wax seal pressed at 45°. Distinctive, memorable, works at all sizes.

Sin Monogram

First letter of سجل as standalone mark. Ultra-minimal. Profile pictures, watermarks, stitching, embossing at micro scale.

02 — WEIGHT VARIATIONS

Hairline to Solid

The same primary seal at four visual weights. Hairline for premium print, solid for high-impact environments.

Hairline

Stroke only, 0.4px. Engraving, embossing, luxury print on cream stock.

Light

Reduced opacity fills. Elegant presence on dark backgrounds, secondary applications.

Regular

Standard weight. Primary usage. Business cards, app icons, all digital interfaces.

Bold

Heavy weight. Signage, large-format print, contexts requiring maximum visual authority.

03 — MONOCHROME VERSIONS

Single Color Applications

For contexts requiring single-color reproduction: legal printing, fax headers, embossing, co-branding.

Brass on Ink

Primary brand expression. Maximum authority.

Ink on Brass

Inverted. Premium materials: brass plaques, special edition prints.

Parchment on Ink

Warm white. Technical documentation, co-branding, neutral contexts.

Outline

Stroke-only. Watermarks, background textures, screen-print on fabric.

04 — CONSTRUCTION GRID

Geometric Foundation

The octagonal seal is built on an 8-point construction with golden ratio proportions. The inner circle sits at φ ratio to the outer bound. The Arabic text center aligns with the optical center, not the mathematical center.

10 — MOTION REFERENCE

Animation Principles

Three animations. All are slow, authoritative, never playful. A register does not bounce. It settles.

Seal Glow

4s ease-in-out infinite. Drop shadow pulses between 15px and 40px brass opacity. Used on hero and idle splash state.

Ring Spin

30s linear infinite. Outer dashed ring rotates slowly. Suggests continuous recording — the register never stops. Loading and "system active" state.

Seal Float

5s ease-in-out infinite. ±6px vertical. Slow, gravity-aware. Hero section only — never inside the working POS interface.

ANIMATION RULES
DURATION
Minimum 3 seconds per cycle. No fast animations. Authority is measured in seconds, not milliseconds.
EASING
ease-in-out only. No bounce, no spring, no elastic. The register settles — it does not bounce.
CONTEXT
Hero, splash, and marketing only. Inside the active POS — no animations. Static seal. The restaurant doesn't need theater.
HAPTIC PAIR
Stamp animation (.notificationOccurred(.success)). Single impact, not a pulse. Matches the seal-press metaphor exactly.
